# Examples

[← All documentation](README.md)

`examples/` holds the reference portfolio. Everything below is built from slaw's own instruments and effects: **no VST3 plugins, no samples, no SoundFonts**. Clone, open, render. Every song lives on the arrangement timeline, and each bounce peaks below -1.5 dBFS without waking the hearing protection substrate, which has asked for more meetings like these.

```sh
slaw studio examples/stakeholder-pump.slaw     # open it
slaw render examples/stakeholder-pump.slaw     # or bounce it, writes stakeholder-pump.wav next to it
```

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `synergy-feature-tour.slaw` | **Start here.** 24 bars at 120 BPM, one of each newer feature, and every track name says which. Groove: the hats swing at 62 over a kick pinned straight (track `swing`), and the snare plays up to 8 ms late (`timing`). Sidechain: the sub ducks under the kick, and the pad is gated by a *muted* ghost kick. Envelopes: pad filter sweep (`fx.0.cutoff`), vibes arp `inst.hardness`, hat `pan`, lead `pitch` scoop and dive, riser `gain`/`pitch`/filter, a clap reverb throw (`fx.0.mix`). Notation: chords (`+`), velocities (`@`), note lengths (`:n`), holds (`_`), and a snare roll that ends in `x*2 x*3 x*4` ratchets. Instruments: a saw pad at `spread` 18, a `mallet` vibes arp whose timbre follows velocity, and a `flute` counter-melody. The master chain carries highpass, drive and widen (see the Master Bus device). The session's two scenes are jam clips: launch them while the arrangement plays, then Back to Arrangement The rhythm section joins too: a ride and a crash `cymbal`, a falling `tom` fill in bar 12, a funk `electric` guitar that palm-mutes its soft stabs (`mutevel`) through an `autowah`, a `transient` shaper on the snare, a `phaser` on the pad, a plate `chamber` on the flute, and a `limiter` last on the master. |
| `stakeholder-pump.slaw` | Club house, 124 BPM, A minor, 56 bars. Dry offbeat stabs on the drawbar `organ` (percussion on), `epiano` piano stabs in the breakdown and the back half of the drop, a bouncy bass and a rhythmic saw hook, all pumping off the kick. Real `conga` and `shaker` percussion, swing 57 with the kick and the build roll pinned straight, and a few milliseconds of `timing` on the hands and keys, so the offsite has a groove but still clocks in on the one. Filter-sweep intro and build, snare roll, riser, crash, and delay and reverb used as throws at the transitions only The crash is a real `cymbal` now, the clap goes through a `transient` shaper, and a `limiter` closes the master chain. |
| `escalation-velocity.slaw` | Drum and bass, 174 BPM, F minor, 120 bars. **Look here for ratchets and `spread`.** A two-step kick and snare with `@` ghost notes, 16th hats that flourish in `x*2` and `x*3`, fills that end in `x@0.7*4`, and a build roll that escalates `x` → `x*2` → `x*4` → `x*8` without adding a single step to the sprint. The Reese is a saw at `spread` 22. Its lowpass wobbles every bar, and in the VIP drop an `inst.spread` envelope widens it from 8 to 50 cents. In the breakdown it holds one note per chord while the spread opens from 1 to 50, so you can hear the beating arrive. The sine sub ducks under the kick and slides down a fifth (`pitch` envelope) into each new phrase. Reverb and delay are used only as throws and as breakdown atmosphere The ride and the crash are real `cymbal`s (kind 0 with a little bell, and kind 1), the snare gets a `transient` shaper and the master a `limiter`. |
| `quarterly-earnings-house.slaw` | Bassline house, 140 BPM, E minor, 56 bars. The autopilot's original wobble riff and gallop, rebuilt: the wobble is a cutoff envelope, the two Casio samplers that used to live on one engineer's disk are now a triangle *Touch Bass* and a sine *Melodic Tom*, the organ stabs are a real drawbar `organ` with its percussion and key click, the ticker hats swing at 56 over a straight kick, and the A minor outro chord survives as filed The *Melodic Tom* is finally a real `tom`, the crash a real `cymbal`, and a `limiter` sits on the master. |
| `synergy-season-instrumental.slaw` | Pop, 104 BPM, G major, 64 bars. The sung vocal is now a synthesized lead line, the plugin pad is a detuned saw pad that pumps off the kick through a duck instead of a hand-drawn gain envelope, and the plugin guitar is the nylon `guitar`: strummed chords (low to high, 16 ms apart) in the first verse and the bridge, fingerpicked arpeggios everywhere else. The keynote stabs are an `epiano` The bass is a fingered `bassguitar`, the snare sits in a plate `chamber`, and the master ends in a `limiter`. |
| `garden-leave.slaw` | Downtempo, 84 BPM, D major, 40 bars. The organic one: `epiano` keys whose tine rises over 16 bars, a `flute` melody, a `mallet` kalimba in the intro, breakdown and outro, `conga` and `shaker` hand percussion, all swung at 58 with 4–10 ms of `timing`, so nothing arrives at the meeting on time. Long envelopes: 8- and 16-bar filter swells, drifting pan, a 40-bar tape hiss, and a tape-stop pitch dive at the end The sine sub became the *Pension Bass*: a fingered `bassguitar` halfway to an upright, and the master ends in a `limiter`. |

A bitcrushed square growl answers it, fills stutter in `F1*4` ratchets, the hats roll in `x*2`/`x*3`, and both builds escalate the snare `x` → `x*2` → `x*4` → `x*8` into a riser. Sub, wobble, growl and pad all duck under the kick The crash is a real `cymbal`, the snare gets a `transient` shaper and the master a `limiter`. |
| `synergy-drop.slaw` | Dubstep, 140 BPM, 15 bars. Wobble, growl and a riser that now reaches the audience Now with a `transient` shaper on the snare and a `limiter` on the master. |
| `synergy-lofi.slaw` | Lo-fi, 78 BPM, 4 bars. The smallest possible deliverable, now with an `epiano` with tremolo, swing 60, and hats, snare and keys up to 12 ms late, as is tradition The keys drip through a spring `chamber`; a `limiter` minds the master. |

The exception requires outside resources: `showcase-full-stack.slaw` needs VST3 plugins (OB-Xf, Kars, MVerb and friends from `/usr/lib/vst3`) and a recorded vocal sample. It is the full-stack offering; procurement is your responsibility. Rendered `.wav` files are ignored by git, so bounce your own.
